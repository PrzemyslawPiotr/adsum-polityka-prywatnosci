About the App

Adsum is a modern mobile application designed for trip organizers and participants. The app makes planning, managing, and communicating during group trips easier, providing all participants with convenient access to important information and the ability to stay in touch.

Key Features

🗺️

Trip Organization

Create trips, invite participants, and manage all details in one place.

👥

Participant List

View all trip participants and their contact information. Organizers have access to additional details.

💬

Group & Private Chat

Communicate with the whole group or have private conversations with selected participants.

🔒

Data Security

Your data is secure. You can delete it at any time, and the app does not use it for marketing purposes.

Why Adsum?

The Adsum app has been designed with simplicity and functionality in mind. You don't need complicated accounts or lengthy registration processes – just download the app and start using it.

All data is stored securely in the cloud, and you have full control over your information. After a trip ends, all data can be automatically deleted, ensuring complete privacy.
